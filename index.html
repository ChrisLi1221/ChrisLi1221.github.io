About

I study how cities move — and how the tools we use to study movement could be far easier to reach for. My work sits between traffic simulation and language models: building agents that can set up, run, and interpret a microscopic simulation from a plain description of the scenario, so that testing a signal retiming or a demand shift is a question you ask rather than a week of configuration.

I am advised by Prof. Ruimin Ke.

Before an agent can answer that question well, the underlying model has to be right. So a good part of my time goes to the unglamorous half — network extraction, demand calibration, and deciding which measures of effectiveness actually tell you whether an intervention worked.

Research

Intelligent Transportation Systems
Adaptive signal control, connected and automated vehicle operations, and corridor-level management strategies.
LLM Agents for Engineering Workflows
Tool-using agents that translate natural-language intent into reproducible modeling pipelines, with the model's assumptions kept inspectable.
Traffic Simulation & Optimization
Microscopic simulation in SUMO, demand generation and calibration, and optimization over control parameters against delay and throughput.

Projects

ChatSUMO-Agent

Open sourcePython · MIT

A single-agent system that turns plain-English prompts into a full SUMO workflow — network generation, demand creation, simulation runs, and KPI analysis. Modular MCP providers handle each stage, with a Streamlit chat interface on top.
